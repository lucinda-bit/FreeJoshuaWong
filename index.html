What Britain should do

Britain guaranteed Hong Kong’s freedoms in 1984. Ten governments, including our own, signed a joint statement condemning Joshua’s sentence. Now words need to become action.

Our first ask

Summon China’s Ambassador

The Prime Minister should call in the Ambassador and name Joshua and the others jailed for standing up for democracy, as the Government did for Jimmy Lai.

Read the open letter

Review the Hong Kong trade office

Its London office keeps diplomatic-style privileges, even after a senior manager there was convicted of spying on Hong Kongers in Britain.

Sanction those responsible

Hold the officials behind National Security Law prosecutions to account, together with Britain’s allies.
