The case

He kept his word. Will we?

THEN

At fifteen he forced the Hong Kong government to drop a school curriculum written in Beijing. At seventeen he was the face of the Umbrella Movement. Time put him on the cover; Netflix told his story.

Everything he campaigned for came back to one thing: holding Beijing to “One Country, Two Systems”, the promise Britain negotiated and guaranteed in 1984.

NOW

He has been behind bars since November 2020. He was due out in January 2027. Instead, in June 2025, he was charged again under the Beijing-imposed National Security Law, with “conspiring to collude with foreign forces”.

The evidence was his own public words: testimony to parliaments, interviews, op-eds. On 5 November 2026 a Hong Kong court gave him ten more years.

He is serving a sentence for believing us.

1984

Britain gave its word

The UK signed the Joint Declaration and guaranteed Hong Kong’s freedoms for fifty years. The Government has itself declared China in breach of it.

2026

It has reached our streets

Two men were jailed at the Old Bailey this year for spying on Hong Kong dissidents in Britain. Hong Kongers settled here have bounties on their heads.

BN(O)

It is a test of what we stand for

Human beings must come before commercial interests. If Britain stays quiet about Joshua, what is its promise to Hong Kongers worth?

Keep your word

Parliamentarians from every major party, former Foreign Secretaries and leading human rights organisations have written to the Prime Minister.

Open letter to the Prime Minister · September 2026

Britain should stand for something

We are asking you to apply that principle to Hong Kong and the Joint Declaration, and hold China to account for its abusive use of the National Security Law, criminalising peaceful democratic activity.

The UK has a moral and diplomatic responsibility, arising from the 1997 handover and the Joint Declaration, to speak out for those who stood up for the treaty it guaranteed.

It is time that you called the Chinese Ambassador to raise Joshua’s case and that of the others imprisoned for standing up for democracy.

Britain signed the Joint Declaration and guaranteed Hong Kong’s freedoms for fifty years. Joshua Wong only ever asked his government to keep its word. We are asking ours to keep it too.
