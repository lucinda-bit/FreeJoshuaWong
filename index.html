In 2017, Teenager vs. Superpower followed a schoolboy who took on Beijing. This short film tells what happened next.

What Britain should do

Britain guaranteed Hong Kong’s freedoms in 1984. Ten governments, including our own, have condemned Joshua’s sentence. Now words need to become action.

Raise his case with China’s ambassador

The Prime Minister should call in the Ambassador and name Joshua and the others jailed for standing up for democracy.

Review the Hong Kong trade office

Its London office keeps diplomatic-style privileges, even after a senior manager there was convicted of spying on Hong Kongers in Britain.

Sanction those responsible

Hold the officials behind National Security Law prosecutions to account, together with Britain’s allies.
