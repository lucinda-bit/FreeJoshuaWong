What Britain should do

Three asks

Raise Joshua’s case with the Chinese Ambassador

The Prime Minister should call in the Ambassador and raise Joshua and the others imprisoned for standing up for democracy, by name.

Review the Hong Kong trade office’s privileges

The Hong Kong Economic and Trade Office in London keeps diplomatic-style privileges, even after a senior manager there was convicted of spying on Hong Kongers in Britain.

Sanction those responsible

Hold to account the officials behind National Security Law prosecutions, working with Britain’s allies.
