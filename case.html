Who he is

Joshua Wong has campaigned for democracy in Hong Kong since he was a schoolboy. At fifteen he stopped a Beijing-written curriculum; at seventeen he was the face of the Umbrella Movement; at twenty he was the subject of an award-winning Netflix documentary.

What he is charged with

“Conspiracy to collude with foreign forces” under the National Security Law, a law imposed by Beijing in 2020.

  • The evidence is his own words: testimony to parliaments and human rights bodies, interviews and op-eds.
  • Much of it predates the law: the prosecution relies largely on advocacy from before the law existed.
  • He was already in prison: he was charged from his cell, while still serving another sentence.
  • The result: ten more years, handed down on 5 November 2026.

He is serving a sentence for believing us.

1984

Britain gave its word

The UK signed the Joint Declaration and guaranteed Hong Kong’s freedoms for fifty years. The Government has itself declared China in breach of it.

2026

It has reached our streets

Two men were jailed at the Old Bailey this year for spying on Hong Kong dissidents in Britain. Hong Kongers settled here have bounties on their heads.

BN(O)

It is a test of what we stand for

Human beings must come before commercial interests. If Britain stays quiet about Joshua, what is its promise to Hong Kongers worth?

His life so far

From 15 to 40

Oct 1996Born

Born in Hong Kong

Joshua Wong Chi-fung grows up in a city promised a high degree of autonomy until 2047.

Sep 2012Age 15

Defeats a Beijing curriculum

With the student group Scholarism, which he co-founded at 14, he leads the protests that force the Hong Kong government to shelve a compulsory “national education” curriculum.

Sep 2014Age 17

The Umbrella Movement

His arrest at Civic Square helps spark 79 days of street occupation demanding genuine universal suffrage. Time puts him on its cover.

Oct 2016Age 19

Detained at Bangkok airport

Held and deported by Thai authorities on his way to speak at a university, an early sign of how far Beijing’s pressure would reach.

2017Age 20

First prison sentence, and the film

Jailed over the 2014 Civic Square protest; Hong Kong’s top court later quashes the sentence. Joshua: Teenager vs. Superpower premieres at Sundance and wins the audience award.

Sep 2019Age 22

Testifies to the US Congress

Gives evidence to the Congressional-Executive Commission on China on human rights in Hong Kong. Years later, testimony like this is cited as evidence against him.

Jun 2020Age 23

The National Security Law

Beijing imposes the National Security Law on Hong Kong. His party, Demosistō, disbands the same day.

Nov 2020Age 24

Into prison, where he remains

Jailed over a 2019 protest outside police headquarters. He has not been free since.

Jan 2021Age 24

Charged as one of the Hong Kong 47

Arrested under the National Security Law for taking part in an unofficial pro-democracy primary election.

Nov 2024Age 28

Sentenced to 4 years 8 months

Sentenced in the largest national security trial in Hong Kong’s history. His release date is set for early 2027.

Jun 2025Age 28

Charged again from his cell

Charged with conspiracy to collude with foreign forces, carrying a maximum of life imprisonment. The evidence is his own advocacy and public statements.

Sep 2026Age 29

Raised in Parliament, then a guilty plea

On 1 September Sir Iain Duncan Smith raises his case in the Commons and the Foreign Secretary says he will take it up. The next day Joshua pleads guilty. On 13 September, 31 parliamentarians and human rights leaders write to the Prime Minister.

13 Oct 2026Age 30

Turns thirty in prison

Parliamentarians mark his birthday with a screening of the new film in the Houses of Parliament.

5 Nov 2026Age 30

Ten more years

A Hong Kong court sentences him to ten years. Ten governments, including the UK, condemn the sentence the next day.

10 Nov 2026Age 30

Raised again in the Commons

Five days after the sentence, Emily Darlington MP asks the Government to summon the Chinese Ambassador and call for Joshua’s release.

c. 2036Age 40

Release, if nothing changes

Unless governments act, Joshua may not walk free until he is around 40, having spent most of his adult life in prison.
