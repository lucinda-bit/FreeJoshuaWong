Joshua Wong: fact sheet

Updated 13 November 2026
Who
Joshua Wong Chi-fung, born 13 October 1996, Hong Kong. Democracy campaigner since age 14; leader of the 2012 campaign against a Beijing-backed “national education” curriculum and a face of the 2014 Umbrella Movement.
In prison since
November 2020, continuously.
Latest sentence
Ten years, handed down 5 November 2026, for “conspiracy to collude with foreign forces” under the National Security Law.
Previous release date
January 2027, at the end of his 4 year 8 month sentence in the Hong Kong 47 case.
Possible release
Around 2036, when he will be about 40.
The evidence
His own advocacy: meetings with foreign politicians, social media posts, op-eds and interviews, much of it from before the law existed.
International response
Joint statement of 6 November 2026 by Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, the Netherlands, New Zealand, the UK and the US condemning the sentence. Taiwan and the EU issued their own statements.
Why the UK
The UK signed the 1984 Joint Declaration guaranteeing Hong Kong’s freedoms for fifty years, and has declared China in breach of it.
The asks
Raise Joshua’s case with the Chinese Ambassador; review the privileges of the Hong Kong Economic and Trade Office in London; sanction officials responsible for NSL prosecutions, with allies.
