Joint statement · 10 foreign ministries

Ten governments, including the UK, condemn the sentence

UKUSAustraliaCanadaFranceGermanyItalyJapanNetherlandsNew Zealand

The day after sentencing, the foreign ministries said the case shows how the National Security Law is used to target people for political expression. They called for an end to unjust NSL prosecutions and for the rights promised under the 1984 Sino-British Joint Declaration to be honoured.
